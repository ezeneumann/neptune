Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Value Recalibration: Even premium-forward categories are emphasizing accessible value and essential product benefits, driven by persistent consumer sensitivity to price changes and inflation fatigue.
  • Return to Core & Nostalgia: Consumer preference is shifting toward trusted core SKUs and familiar formats, especially when “newstalgia” packaging or tie-ins are present.
  • AI-Powered Engagement: New investment in applied AI and automation is reshaping search, advertising, and workflow tools, focusing on measurable improvements rather than experimentation for its own sake.
  • Convenience as Differentiator: Innovations around digital service, real-time information, and low-friction tools (seen in airline, retail, and CPG) are standing out as brand loyalty drivers.
  • B2B Purchase Complexity: In professional and office categories, buying cycles are extending, with more collaborative decision-making and increased scrutiny on post-purchase impact.
