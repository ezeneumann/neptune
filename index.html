Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Value Reframing: Economic uncertainty is prompting brands to more directly communicate cost savings, efficiency, and long-term value, underpinned by messaging that reassures rather than excites.
  • Nostalgia & 'Newstalgia': Leveraging heritage brands and retro products as an emotional anchor is driving engagement, particularly in food, beverage, and CPG, with positive feedback from both mature and younger cohorts.
  • Smart Convenience: “Smart” features and seamless experiences (AI, device integrations, shoppable content) are climbing in consumer and B2B priority, but adoption is gated by perceived complexity and privacy concerns.
  • Functionalization: Elevated interest in products promising functional benefits—health, nutrition, energy efficiency—is shaping NPD pipelines across food, beverage, and appliances.
  • Test-and-Learn Agility: Brands are piloting fast A/B tests and in-market pilots (especially online), reflecting risk-averse climates and the need for early read signals before larger-scale rollouts.
