Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Nostalgia and Newstalgia: Brands are reviving classic products and designs, aiming to capture both older consumers’ sentimental value and Gen Z’s interest in “retro” authenticity. This is driven by intensified competition and consumer desire for comfort amid economic flux.
  • Premiumization with Purpose: Even as value-seeking behavior persists, there is continued uptake of premium offerings with clear functional or experiential benefits—especially in travel, foodservice, and personal care.
  • Hybrid Experiences and Smart Enablement: Consumers now expect seamless integration between digital and physical touchpoints, highlighted by smart appliances and enhanced in-store/airport experiences.
  • AI-Enabled Personalization: Marketing teams are accelerating adoption of AI for tailored messaging and offers, prompted by mounting evidence of higher engagement rates and increased cross-platform journeys.
  • Selective B2B Spend: B2B buyers show an increased appetite for solutions that directly reduce operating friction and measurable inefficiency, fueling interest in collaborative technology pilots.
