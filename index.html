  • Return to Familiar, With a Twist: “Nostalgic” and “modern classic” cues remain a strong lever in food, beverage, and lifestyle categories, yet resonating best when paired with better-for-you or functional updates.
  • AI-Enabled Experiences: Early but meaningful commercial experiments in AI-generated content, customer interfaces, and B2B automation are changing expectation benchmarks, especially for digital-native audiences.
  • Elevated B2B Expectations: B2B buyers now expect nearly frictionless, largely digital procurement and personalization. Investment activity is clustered around automated ordering, real-time service, and ROI demonstration.
  • These trends are driven by sustained inflation pressure, platform innovation, and converging category playbooks aiming to win both loyalty and trial in a more selective economy.
