Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Frictionless Experiences: Brands are doubling down on removing pain points and shortening decision cycles, especially in foodservice, travel, and retail. Digital integration and fast, personalized service are must-haves.
  • Functionality & Value Prioritization: Consumers are signaling caution on discretionary spending, gravitating toward multi-benefit, health-forward, or reliably essential products, especially in CPG and beverage.
  • AI-Driven Engagement: Accelerated investment in AI is driving smarter personalization in advertising, product recommendations, and enterprise service models. B2B buyers, too, are valuing efficiency through automation.
  • Loyalty & Rewards: Loyalty programs are in the spotlight as differentiation levers in crowded markets—program mechanics and perceived value are under greater scrutiny from value-conscious consumers.
  • Sustainable Storytelling: Environmental claims are central to CPG brand communications, but shoppers are demanding proof and meaningful action, prompting a shift from “greenwashing” to credible sustainability initiatives.
