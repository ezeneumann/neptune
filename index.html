Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Value-Seeking & Trade-Down Behavior: Mid-tier and lower-income segments are trading down across durables and family essentials, prompting brands to adapt positioning and invest in promo activity.
  • AI-Driven Customer Experiences: Brands are balancing aggressive deployment of AI features with increased consumer/buyer concerns over data privacy and reliability, especially in digital service and content delivery.
  • Premiumization Amid Friction: Despite economic softness, select segments (notably travel and food) continue to pay a premium for upgraded, frictionless, or wellness-forward experiences.
  • B2B Consolidation: Larger B2B buyers are favoring fewer, more trusted partners for solutions procurement, highlighting flexibility and risk-sharing as key commercial levers.
  • Digital-Physical Integration: New product and campaign launches are focusing on seamless experiences that bridge in-person and digital touchpoints, leveraging emerging tech to drive engagement.

Implications for SKIM

 

  • Commercial teams should position SKIM’s agile, claims-testing and value-based proposition research as critical tools in a rapidly recalibrating market.
  • Proactively offer digital engagement measurement, privacy sentiment, and adoption barrier research to accounts launching or expanding AI-enabled or immersive digital experiences.
