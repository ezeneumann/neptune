Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Hybrid Commerce Experiences: Multiple brands are blurring digital and in-person engagement (e.g., shoppable video, experiential rewards programs), aiming to capture a broader spectrum of customer occasions and retain wallet share across touchpoints.
  • Premiumization with Caution: While value-seeking remains high, selective premiumization—anchored in functionality or experience, not just price—is gaining traction, especially in food, beverage, and home categories.
  • Consumer Fatigue with Sustainability Messaging: Brands report limited enthusiasm for green packaging unless paired with tangible product benefits or cost savings; base expectations are rising, but willingness to pay extra remains flat.
  • B2B Renewal and Office Re-engagement: Office and services suppliers are reactivating promotions and partnership campaigns as hybrid work policies stabilize, leading to measured but noticeable uptick in B2B spend.
  • AI-Enabled Personalization: Pilots in digital media and advertising are deepening; companies are evaluating ROI on personalization tools balanced against privacy and first-party data constraints.
