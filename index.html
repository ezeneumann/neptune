Emerging Themes & Trends

 

  • Nostalgia & “Newstalgia”: Brands are reviving classic products or themes with a modern twist to invoke emotional comfort, especially as shoppers seek familiarity amidst uncertainty.
  • Value Optimization: Consumers are trading down or holding volume steady while switching to lower-priced, private label, or promoted products; premiumization is only holding with clear additive benefits.
  • Elevated Digital Experiences: Across sectors, enhancements in digital commerce, content, and AI-backed personalization are driving engagement and reshaping shopper expectations for convenience and relevance.
  • B2B Risk Caution: In professional categories, buyers are showing increased scrutiny and need for clear, measurable ROI, slowing some new initiative adoption but creating openings for cost-justification research.
